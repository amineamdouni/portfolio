<Section number="01" title="The problem">

People who travel often have spare room in their luggage. People who need a package delivered to another country often pay a lot or wait a long time. DelivAir puts the two in touch: a traveller posts a trip, a sender finds them, they agree in chat and follow the delivery together.

</Section>

<Section number="02" title="What I built">

I built DelivAir as a solo project between November 2022 and January 2023. It is a React Native (Expo) app with a Node and PostgreSQL server, a real-time chat server and a small admin dashboard.

The signature screen is Home: an animated plane flies over clouds while the app searches for travellers between two cities, then shows the results as swipeable cards. Around it sit onboarding, sign-up, trip posting with a flight lookup, a list of requests, chat, delivery tracking, profiles with ratings and reviews, and settings.

</Section>

<Section number="01" title="The problem">

A car business that rents and sells vehicles needs two things: a public site where customers can see the cars, check that one is free on their dates and send a request, and a back office where staff manage the cars, photos and reservations. AMS Cars is that system, built for a client, AMS Dynamic, whose live site is [amsdynamic.fr](https://amsdynamic.fr).

</Section>

<Section number="02" title="What I built">

I built the system as a solo project for the client: a NestJS API, a customer site and an admin dashboard.

**What it covers.**

- The customer site, in French: cars to rent and for sale, filters by category, text and dates, a car page with prices, a photo gallery and an availability check that returns the price from the API, then a rental request that gives you a reference. Cars for sale have a contact button.
- The admin dashboard, in French: log in, see counters, create, edit and delete cars, manage photos (upload, choose the main one, remove), and work through reservations with only the valid status changes allowed.

</Section>

<Section number="04" title="Under the hood">

A pnpm monorepo with `apps/api` (NestJS 11, Prisma 6, PostgreSQL), `apps/site` and `apps/admin` (both Next.js 16, the site with Tailwind 4).

Two decisions worth a look:

- **Pricing as tiers.** A rental is priced in 30-day blocks first, then 7-day blocks, then single days, and each tier is capped at the next one so a longer stay never costs more than a shorter one. Days are counted exclusively with a minimum of 1. I tested the tiers at 1, 5, 6, 7, 8, 13, 14, 29, 30, 31 and 45 days.
- **Booking without double bookings.** Creating a rental takes a row lock and checks for overlaps in the same transaction. A test sends 8 requests at once and expects exactly one to succeed.

Admin sessions use an httpOnly cookie through a same-origin proxy. Each admin has a token version that is bumped on every reset, so tokens issued before a reset stop working.

<Section number="05" title="API and backend">

Routes cover cars (public list and detail, archived cars hidden), availability, rentals (public create, lookup by reference and email, admin list and status changes), contact requests, admin stats and car CRUD, and photo upload. Uploads are limited to 2 MB, JPEG, PNG or WebP checked by file signature, 6 per car and 40 in total, and stored in the database. Throttling is 60 requests a minute per IP across the API, with a stricter 10 a minute on login, booking and contact.

Tests: 17 unit tests for pricing, 57 end-to-end API tests (availability, parallel bookings, auth and forged tokens, car CRUD, upload limits, a 413 on large bodies, CORS, the rate limit), and Playwright tests on the site and the admin (3 each). I also removed a guard on purpose to check the tests fail without it: dropping the month cap, the row lock and the token-purpose check each broke a test. CI runs everything on a Postgres service.

</Section>

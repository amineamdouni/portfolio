<Section title="About this site">

FK-KM is a French-language landing page for a company that sells and installs energy renovation solutions: boilers, heat pumps, ventilation and insulation, each presented with its own short video. I built it for the client as a single-page Next.js site, and the live demo here is a copy of it.

**What is different in the demo copy.** Only the "FK-KM" brand is kept. The contact details, address and map are fake or replaced by a placeholder, personal links and the founders' names are removed, and the contact form is disabled with a visible note and sends nothing. A footer note says it is a demo copy of a client site, built by me. The product videos are compressed to 720p, from 4K originals, to a total of a few megabytes.

**What I did while preparing it.** I upgraded Next.js and React to current versions, fixed the mobile menu that could not be closed with Escape or a tap outside, set the real French title and page language, replaced the default favicon, and made the hero artwork readable on a phone, so the smallest visible text is now 12 px. Two Playwright tests (desktop and mobile) check for console errors and that the form sends no request.

</Section>

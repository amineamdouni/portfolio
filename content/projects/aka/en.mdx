<Section number="02" title="Overview">

AKA is a fashion online shop: a catalogue of clothes, shoes and accessories from well-known brands, a product page with several photos, a cart and an admin area. I built it as a solo project at the end of 2022, with a Next.js front end and an Express server.

</Section>

<Section number="03" title="Under the hood">

A Next.js 13 front end (Pages Router, Bootstrap 5, axios) talking to an Express 4 server with Mongoose and MongoDB, bcrypt for password hashing and JWT for sessions. Products have a name, description, price, colours, sizes, a category and a brand, with three photos each.

**What I would improve.** Tests, environment variables instead of values in code, and a design that does not depend on a template.

</Section>

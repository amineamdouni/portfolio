<Section title="About this site">

Digitalynx is a French-language landing page for a digital marketing and web agency: a hero video, an about section, six service cards, a four-step production process, a quote call to action and a contact section. I built it for the client as a single-page Next.js site, and the live demo here is a copy of it.

**What is different in the demo copy.** The brand name and logo stay, but everything personal is replaced: the phone number, email, social handle and address are fake, the map is a placeholder, and the contact form is disabled with a visible note ("This is a demo copy of a client site; the form is disabled") and sends nothing. The videos are stock footage, and a footer note says the page is a demo copy of a client site, built by me.

**What I did while preparing it.** I upgraded Next.js from 14 to 16 and React from 18 to 19, fixed broken anchors, typos, accessibility errors and the default favicon, set the real French title and page language, and re-encoded the main video from 5.1 MB to 0.7 MB. The hero video used to shift the layout as it loaded; giving it fixed dimensions and a poster took the desktop layout shift from 0.281 to 0.012. Two Playwright tests (desktop and mobile) check for console errors and that the form sends no request.

</Section>

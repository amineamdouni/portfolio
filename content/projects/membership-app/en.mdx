<Section number="01" title="The problem">

A membership product has several kinds of people in one app. Members take courses, join community rooms and talk in voice rooms, and some run their own online stores. Coaches review clients and intake forms. The founder tracks reports and manages the team. Each group needs its own screens, and all of them need to feel like one product on both web and mobile.

</Section>

<Section number="02" title="What I built">

This is a team project, and I worked on it as a freelance full-stack developer from July 2026. My part was online payments, live voice rooms for multiplayer games and player leaderboards, on web and mobile. The product itself belongs to someone else, so this page uses a neutral name, "Membership app".

The app has three roles: Member, Coach and Founder. A member can go through the academy (chapter, lesson, quiz, level-up), the community hub and chat rooms, a voice room, the leaderboard and rewards, fitness coaching with plan selection, and their stores and orders. A coach reviews clients and approves an intake form. A founder resolves reports and invites a team member. English and Arabic are both available, including right-to-left layout.

</Section>
